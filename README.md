# WhyTarun

**A professional mind. A personal world.**

WhyTarun is a personal editorial website connecting professional work, research, technology, projects, interests, experiences, and stories. It is designed to feel like a thoughtfully curated digital space—not a conventional résumé or template portfolio.


## Design direction

**Personal Editorial × Dusk Room** — 70% editorial clarity, 30% warm atmospheric character. Parchment, espresso, walnut, muted olive, antique gold, lavender, and dusty rose; expressive serif headlines with clean sans-serif body typography; deliberate whitespace, asymmetry, fine borders, and restrained translucent surfaces.

## Content integrity

Personal and professional content must be sourced, provenance-tagged, and approved before publication. Never invent achievements, dates, book ownership, travel details, playlist tracklists, or outcomes. Protect employer/client confidentiality.
